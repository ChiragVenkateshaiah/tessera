## The Four Gates

One screen, for use before a steering committee. Full reasoning lives in the market entry methodology pages.

| # | Gate | Passes when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Success bar agreed | Client has stated a revenue or share threshold in writing | "We'll know it when we see it" |
| 2 | Size triangulated | Top-down and bottom-up within ~30% | Single-method estimate only |
| 3 | White space real | An identified, defensible position exists | Every segment is contested by a stronger incumbent |
| 4 | Capability credible | Distribution, regulatory, and brand gaps are closable in the time available | The gap needs a capability the client has never built |

## Disqualifying Conditions

Any one of these is a no-go regardless of how the gates score:

- Regulatory approval timeline exceeds the client's stated strategic window.
- The sizing case depends entirely on an assumption no one on the team can defend.
- Entry requires a partner, and no willing partner of adequate quality exists.

If gates pass but one condition bites, the answer is usually "wait" or "enter a sub-segment first" — not a clean no. Take the conditional recommendation to the committee rather than forcing a binary.
