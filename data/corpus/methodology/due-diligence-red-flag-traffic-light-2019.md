## Overview

The traffic-light report rates every diligence workstream red, amber or green each week and compiles the ratings into the final diligence report as a red flag appendix. It was Meridian's standard red flag format until 2019, valued for giving the deal team one consistent visual across workstreams.

## When to Use It

Used throughout commercial and financial due diligence, with the weekly traffic-light pack sent to the deal team every Friday and the full set of red flags presented in the final report.

## Framework

1. **Rate each workstream weekly.** Each workstream lead assigns a red, amber or green rating to their area every Friday, based on their judgment of the findings to date.
2. **Compile the weekly pack.** Combine the ratings into a one-page traffic-light grid with a short comment per workstream.
3. **Hold findings for the final report.** Detailed red flag findings, with supporting analysis, are written up in the red flag appendix of the final diligence report so the deal team sees the complete picture at once.
4. **Present without a recommendation.** The report states the findings neutrally; the deal team decides what they mean for price and structure.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Inconsistent colors.** Different workstream leads interpret amber differently; agree examples at kickoff.
- **Late packs.** Send the weekly pack by Friday noon so it reaches the deal team before their weekly call.
