## Overview

The decision package method runs zero-based budgeting through a central finance committee: every cost center writes decision packages describing its activities at three funding levels, and the committee ranks all packages against a top-down savings target. It was Meridian's standard ZBB approach for retail SG&A work until 2019.

## When to Use It

Used when a client's executive team had already set a savings target (typically 10-15% of SG&A) and wanted a structured process for deciding where the cuts would land.

## Framework

1. **Set the savings target.** The executive team fixes an across-the-board reduction target for SG&A before the exercise begins.
2. **Write decision packages.** Each cost center manager writes a decision package for every activity, at three funding levels: minimum (the level below which the activity cannot run), current, and enhanced.
3. **Rank in the finance committee.** A central finance committee ranks every decision package across the organization, funding packages in rank order until the savings target is met. Packages below the funding line are cut.
4. **Roll forward.** The funded packages become next year's budget baseline; the full decision-package exercise is repeated every three years.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Package volume.** Large organizations can generate thousands of decision packages; cap each cost center at ten.
- **Ranking fatigue.** Committee ranking sessions should be limited to half a day each.
