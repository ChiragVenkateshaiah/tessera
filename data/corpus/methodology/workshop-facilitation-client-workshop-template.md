## Overview

A standard agenda and facilitation guide for a half-day client workshop — typically used to align a steering group on problem definition, options or a draft recommendation.

## When to Use It

Use when a decision needs input from several senior stakeholders at once and a sequence of one-to-one meetings would take too long or let positions harden.

## Framework

1. **Pre-reads, sent 48 hours ahead.** One page on the decision being sought, the options, and the evidence. Never present new data for the first time in the room.
2. **Opening (15 minutes).** The sponsor states the decision the workshop must reach and the constraints that are not up for debate.
3. **Structured discussion (90 minutes).** Work through the options one at a time with a visible scoring grid; the facilitator keeps time and parks off-topic issues on a visible list.
4. **Decision and owners (30 minutes).** Record the decision, the open questions, and a named owner and date for each.
5. **Write-up within 24 hours.** Circulate the decision log, not a transcript.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Too many attendees.** Above twelve, discussion turns into presentations. Split into breakout groups.
- **No decision framed.** A workshop without a stated decision becomes a status update.

## Related Frameworks

Change management — stakeholder mapping (who must be in the room), communication planning (how the outcome is shared).
