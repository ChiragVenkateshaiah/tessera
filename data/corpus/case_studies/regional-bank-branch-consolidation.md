## Situation

A Midwestern regional bank with around 400 branches and a cost-income ratio well above its peers needed a multi-year cost programme that protected lending capacity.

## What We Did

We combined zero-based budgeting of corporate cost pools with a branch-level profitability model and a spans-and-layers review of operations.

## Result

The bank committed to roughly $300m of run-rate savings, including the consolidation of nearly a hundred overlapping branches with minimal deposit attrition.
