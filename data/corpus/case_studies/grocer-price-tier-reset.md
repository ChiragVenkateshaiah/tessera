## Situation

A leading UK grocer with around 2,800 stores found that its value own-label tier had drifted to within a few percent of branded equivalents on a large share of key value items.

## What We Did

We estimated item-level price elasticities from two years of loyalty-card data, grouped stores into price zones by competitive intensity, and rebuilt the good/better/best ladder.

## Result

Re-opening the tier gaps recovered several hundred million pounds of annual gross margin at flat volume, and convenience-store price zones were simplified.
