## Client Context

Project Foxglove was a four-week commercial due diligence for a private equity fund bidding on a European rigid plastic packaging manufacturer with €1.2bn revenue and 19 plants, at an indicated enterprise value of 9.5x EBITDA.

## Scope

Test the management case of 6% annual revenue growth, assess exposure to packaging regulation, and flag anything material to price or structure.

## Approach

Customer interviews with 42 of the top 60 accounts, a plant-by-plant capacity and utilization review, and a regulatory scenario model for the EU packaging waste regulation's recycled-content mandates.

## Findings

- Red flag: the top customer, at 17% of revenue, was dual-sourcing a new product line with a competitor from the next contract renewal.
- The 6% growth case relied on a 2.5-point price increase that customer interviews did not support; the team's base case was 3.4%.
- Recycled-content mandates required an estimated €140m of capital expenditure by 2030 that was not in the management plan.

## Outcome

The fund reduced its bid and negotiated a specific indemnity tied to the top-customer contract renewal.
