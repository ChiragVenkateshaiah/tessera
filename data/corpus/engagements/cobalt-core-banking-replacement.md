## Client Context

Project Cobalt advised a Nordic retail bank with 2.3 million customers on replacing a 1980s mainframe core banking system whose vendor support would end in 2028.

## Scope

Select between three vendor platforms, define the migration approach, and build the business case for the board.

## Approach

A capability assessment against 380 requirements, reference calls with nine banks that had completed migrations, and a migration risk model comparing big-bang and progressive approaches.

## Findings

- A progressive, product-by-product migration over 30 months carried a third of the outage risk of a big-bang cutover, at 12% higher total cost.
- The shortlisted cloud-native vendor scored highest on capability but had no reference client above 1 million customers.
- Total programme cost was estimated at €410m, with run-cost savings of €55m a year from year four.

## Outcome

The board approved the progressive approach, starting with savings accounts, and required a scale proof-of-concept from the cloud-native vendor before contract signature.
