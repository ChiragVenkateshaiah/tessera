## Client Context

Project Osprey built a decarbonization roadmap for an integrated steelmaker with two blast furnace sites producing 9.6 million tonnes a year, facing a 2035 deadline under its sustainability-linked loan covenants.

## Scope

Compare decarbonization pathways, sequence the capital plan, and test the economics under carbon price scenarios.

## Approach

Site-level abatement cost curves, a pathway model comparing electric arc furnace conversion with hydrogen direct reduction, and carbon price scenarios from €60 to €160 a tonne.

## Findings

- Converting the smaller site to an electric arc furnace by 2031 delivered 41% of the required abatement at the lowest cost per tonne.
- Hydrogen direct reduction at the larger site was only economic above €120 a tonne of carbon unless green hydrogen fell below €3 a kilogram.
- Scrap availability, not capital, was the binding constraint on the electric arc pathway.

## Outcome

The board approved the electric arc conversion and a scrap-sourcing joint venture, and deferred the hydrogen decision to 2027.
