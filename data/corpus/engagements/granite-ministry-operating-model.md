## Client Context

Project Granite redesigned the operating model of a national transport ministry with 7,200 staff across 14 regional directorates, following a spending review that required a 15% reduction in administrative cost.

## Scope

Design the target operating model, rationalize the regional structure, and clarify decision rights between the centre and the regions.

## Approach

An activity analysis across 210 teams, a decision-rights matrix for 64 recurring decisions, and option appraisal of three regional structures.

## Findings

- Thirty-one percent of regional activity duplicated central policy work.
- Consolidating 14 directorates into five regional hubs met the 15% target without reducing road inspection capacity.
- Capital approval decisions passed through an average of seven sign-offs; the redesigned matrix reduced that to three.

## Outcome

The ministry adopted the five-hub model, implemented over two budget years, and published the new decision-rights framework internally.
