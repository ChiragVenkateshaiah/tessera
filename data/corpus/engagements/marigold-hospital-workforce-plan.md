## Client Context

Project Marigold built a five-year workforce plan for a hospital system in the US Southeast with nine hospitals, 3,600 beds and a nursing vacancy rate of 14%.

## Scope

Forecast clinical workforce demand, close the nursing gap, and reduce reliance on agency staff.

## Approach

Demand forecasting by service line and unit, a nursing supply model covering pipeline, attrition and retirement, and a cost comparison of agency staffing against an internal float pool.

## Findings

- Agency nursing cost $212m a year, 2.4x the cost of equivalent employed hours.
- An internal float pool of 450 nurses would cut agency spend by $95m a year within three years.
- First-year nurse attrition of 27% was the largest controllable driver of the gap; a structured residency programme was projected to halve it.

## Outcome

The system launched the float pool and a nurse residency programme, with agency spend reduced by a third in the first year.
