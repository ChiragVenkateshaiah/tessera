## Client Context

Project Juniper redesigned the distribution network of an Australian home-improvement retailer with 290 stores, seven distribution centres and a fast-growing online channel shipping 40,000 orders a week.

## Scope

Recommend the target network for 2030, including the location and role of any new facilities, and quantify the cost-to-serve impact.

## Approach

A network optimization model across 31 candidate sites, store-level demand forecasts to 2030, and a cost-to-serve analysis splitting bulky and small-parcel flows.

## Findings

- Bulky goods were driving 58% of transport cost from 22% of volume; a dedicated bulky-goods DC near the Western Sydney airport corridor cut that by a quarter.
- Two of the seven DCs could be closed and their volume absorbed, saving A$38m a year.
- Ship-from-store for small parcels in regional areas was cheaper than any DC option.

## Outcome

The client approved the bulky-goods DC and the two closures, phased over three years.
