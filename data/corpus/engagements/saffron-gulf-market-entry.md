## Client Context

Project Saffron assessed entry into Saudi Arabia and the UAE for a European fast-fashion retailer with 1,400 stores in 30 countries and no presence in the Gulf.

## Scope

Size the opportunity, recommend an entry mode, and identify a launch city and store format.

## Approach

Bottom-up market sizing from mall footfall and household apparel spend, interviews with five regional franchise groups, and a store-economics model for flagship and mall formats.

## Findings

- The addressable fast-fashion market across both countries was sized at $4.7bn, growing 7% a year.
- A franchise partnership outperformed a joint venture on risk-adjusted returns because prime mall leases were controlled by three franchise groups.
- Riyadh, not Dubai, was the recommended launch city: lower competitive density and a younger customer base.

## Outcome

The client signed a ten-year franchise agreement covering 45 stores and opened its first Riyadh store the following year.
