## Client Context

Project Tamarind supported the merger of two North American salty-snack manufacturers, a $3.4bn acquirer and a $1.1bn family-owned target, combining 11 plants and two direct-store-delivery networks.

## Scope

Stand up the integration management office, plan Day 1, and validate the $180m synergy case announced to investors.

## Approach

An IMO with nine workstreams, a Day 1 critical path of 240 must-have items, and a bottom-up synergy validation by plant, route and SKU.

## Findings

- Distribution synergies were overstated: route overlap supported $45m, not the $70m announced, because the two DSD networks served different store formats.
- Procurement synergies of $60m were achievable on corn, oils and film packaging.
- The target's founder-led sales culture was the main retention risk; 30% of the target's top route managers were approached by competitors in the first month.

## Outcome

Day 1 closed with no payroll or ordering disruptions. The revised synergy case of $165m was communicated to investors at the first post-close earnings call.
