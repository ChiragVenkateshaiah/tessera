## Client Context

Project Aurora supported a Canadian property and casualty insurer with 4.5 million policies in scaling generative AI from pilots to production in claims and underwriting.

## Scope

Prioritize use cases, design the governance and model-risk controls, and build the value case for the next 24 months.

## Approach

Assessment of 37 pilot use cases on value, feasibility and risk, a model-risk framework aligned with the regulator's guidance, and an adoption plan for 3,100 claims adjusters.

## Findings

- Claims first notice of loss summarization cut adjuster handling time by 23% in the pilot region and was ready to scale.
- Underwriting submission triage for commercial lines carried the highest value but required human review of every declination to meet fairness requirements.
- Twenty-two of the 37 pilots had no measurable baseline and were stopped.

## Outcome

The insurer scaled two use cases nationally and established a model-risk committee reporting to the chief risk officer.
