# Project Tessera — Discovery Workshop Questionnaire

**Purpose:** working question set for the 90-minute kickoff workshop with Priya Anand (VP, Knowledge & Capability) and the Meridian Advisory team.
**Status:** internal working document — used to run the discovery session. Superseded downstream by `Tessera_Discovery_Findings.md`.

Each question asks one thing. Priorities are noted where a thread is load-bearing; not all questions were expected to fit in 90 minutes.

---

## The Failed Attempt — What Went Wrong and Why

*(Highest priority. The prior attempt is the single richest source of signal in the engagement.)*

1. What specifically went wrong with the previous tool — was the core issue relevance, hallucinations, speed, UI, or something else entirely?
2. Who championed the previous initiative internally?
3. Who killed it — and are any of the partners who rejected it still influential in this decision?
4. When a consultant can't find what they need today, what's the actual workaround? Do they Slack a colleague, email a partner, dig through folders for an hour, or rebuild from scratch?

---

## Users, Workflows & Real Query Patterns

5. Who is the primary user — juniors, managers, partners, or all three?
6. Do different seniority levels search differently? (e.g., a junior looking for a reusable framework slide vs. a partner asking who in the firm has pharma M&A experience)
7. Beyond the failed examples from the old tool, what are the 10–15 most common types of questions consultants ask each other in Slack DMs or hallway conversations?
8. Who is the "skeptical partner" you're bringing to the workshop, and what would specifically change their mind?

---

## Data, Scope & Access Control

9. How are NDAs and ethical walls enforced today — is there a formal system of record that maps which consultants are cleared for which clients, or is it handled manually?
10. Who maintains that client-consultant access mapping, and how frequently is it updated?
11. What's the real cost of an accidental data leak — internal embarrassment, a lawsuit, or losing the client relationship entirely?
12. How well-organized and tagged are the 15,000 PowerPoint decks — structured folders with consistent naming, or closer to chaos?
13. Do the decks follow standard Meridian templates with detailed speaker notes, or are they highly customized layouts where the knowledge is locked inside diagrams and graphics?
14. Is Slack in scope or out? This was hedged in the brief — we need a clear boundary before scoping the ingestion pipeline.
15. How fresh does the content need to be? If a consultant uploads a new deck at 2 PM, does the assistant need to surface it by 3 PM, or is a nightly sync acceptable?

---

## Success Criteria, Launch Strategy & Decision-Making

16. You mentioned 50% weekly adoption, NPS above 30, and time-to-information cut in half. How will these be measured — is the instrumentation already in place, or does it need to be built?
17. What does "launch with credibility" mean concretely — a curated pilot group, a partners-only beta, a soft launch to one office, or something else?
18. Who makes the final go/no-go decision to ship — you alone, a partner committee, or the CTO?
19. What happens if adoption is at 20% after month two — do we iterate, or does it get shelved like the last attempt? What's the kill criterion?
20. "Reasonable" budget means different things to different people. Can you share a rough range — are we in proof-of-concept territory or production-platform territory?

---

## Facilitation notes

- 20 questions will not fit in 90 minutes. Expect to cover 12–15 live.
- Never drop: the failed-attempt section, and Q8 (the skeptical partner).
- First to drop if time runs short: Q12, Q13, Q15 — these are answerable by email afterwards.
- Ask one question at a time. Let answers land before advancing.
